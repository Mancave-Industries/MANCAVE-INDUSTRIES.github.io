Commissions

Got a can that means something?

The beer from your stag do, your wedding, the brewery you found on holiday. Send it over and it comes back as a Mangrenade.

  1. Email us and tell us about the can.
  2. Rinse it, post it, or drop it at the market stall.
  3. We stencil, spray and pour. It comes back boxed.

Originals

Built different

Spray paint, stencils, resin and found objects, each one of a kind, painted to change under a blacklight. See them at the market stall, or email mancaveman@icloud.com to arrange a viewing.

The maker

Built in the shed

Mancave Industries is Mark's shed: a spray booth, a wax pot, a UV light and a lot of empty cans. It's where the Mangrenades get made, the artwork gets layered up, and the Game Shed puzzles get built.
